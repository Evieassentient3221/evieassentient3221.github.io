🎮 What is save-sync?

save-sync is a user-friendly tool that automatically backs up and syncs your retro gaming save files to the cloud. It works perfectly with popular retro gaming systems like Batocera, KNULLI, and Recalbox. Whether you're playing on an Anbernic RG35XX, a Trimui Brick, a Raspberry Pi, or any other supported handheld, save-sync ensures your progress is always safe and accessible.

Think of it as a safety net for your gaming memories. No more losing hours of progress because an SD card failed or a device was lost. With save-sync, your saves are automatically uploaded to your preferred cloud storage (WebDAV, rclone-compatible services, and more).
